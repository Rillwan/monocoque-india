Privacy Policy.

Last updated: September 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Monocoque Pvt Ltd ("Monocoque", "we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through monocoqueindia.com, how we use it, and the choices you have.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, phone number, and any message content when you fill out our contact form or career application form, including resume files you upload. We may also collect limited technical data (browser type, device, pages visited) through standard analytics tools to understand site usage.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected to respond to enquiries, evaluate job applications, provide requested services, and improve our website. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

4. Job Applications

Resumes and cover letters submitted through our Careers page are used solely for recruitment purposes and are retained only as long as necessary to evaluate your application, unless you consent to us keeping your details on file for future opportunities.

5. Cookies & Tracking

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies for basic functionality and analytics. You can disable cookies through your browser settings; some site features may not function as intended if you do so.

6. Data Sharing

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate our website or process applications (such as hosting or email delivery services), under confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information where required by law.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information against unauthorised access, alteration, or loss. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information held by us by contacting us at the details below.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

10. Contact Us

For any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at sales@monocoqueindia.com or +91 90725 09773.
