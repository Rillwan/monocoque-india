About Us

About Monocoque Pvt Ltd

Precision, Performance, Offshore Excellence. Navigating Solutions Beyond the Horizon.

Monocoque Pvt Ltd is a trusted service provider in the marine and oil & gas sectors, offering world-class solutions for vessel construction, repair, maintenance, and offshore support services. The company has been in operation for over 23 years and has a team of highly skilled professionals, including international-standard welders and technicians, who provide a range of services to clients in the marine industry.

The company offers a range of services including new building vessels like cargo vessels, landing crafts, water barges, fuel barges, supply vessels, tug boats, fishing vessels, and other related seagoing & coastal vessels. It also offers services like hull repairs, mechanical and electrical repairs, painting and blasting, and fabrication and welding jobs.

Monocoque Pvt Ltd Ship Building & Repair and Slipway Dockyard has a reputation for providing high-quality services and has worked with a range of clients including commercial shipping companies, offshore oil and gas companies. The company is committed to providing a safe and environmentally friendly service and has implemented a range of measures to reduce its impact on the environment.

Our Vision & Mission

Monocoque Pvt Ltd envisions becoming a leading force in the marine and offshore industries by delivering exceptional vessel construction, repair, and support services. With over two decades of experience, the company is driven by a mission to serve clients with innovative, reliable, and cost-effective solutions that meet global standards. Through its commitment to continuous improvement, skilled workforce, and customer-centric approach, Monocoque aims to play a vital role in shaping the future of marine engineering and offshore support services.

Core Values

At the heart of Monocoque Pvt Ltd is a dedication to quality, integrity, and technical excellence. The company values its team of highly trained professionals—international-standard welders, technicians, and engineers—who uphold rigorous standards in every project. By fostering long-term partnerships, embracing innovation, and staying committed to timely delivery and dependable service, Monocoque reinforces its position as a trusted partner in the marine and oil & gas sectors.
