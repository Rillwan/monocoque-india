Who We Are

An engineering and manufacturing solutions company.

Monocoque Pvt Ltd delivers precision-engineered products and services for demanding industrial applications across Marine & Shipbuilding, Oil & Gas, Aerospace & Defence, Automotive, Construction Equipment and Industrial Engineering.

Our capabilities include engineering design and analysis, precision machining, heavy structural fabrication, welding, casting and forging, piping solutions, composite structures, industrial components, repair and maintenance, and customised manufacturing.

We turn engineering requirements into practical, manufacturable and cost-effective solutions, from concept and design through manufacturing, inspection, testing and delivery, with a strong commitment to quality, safety, precision and reliability.

We follow recognised engineering practices and applicable international standards, and our multidisciplinary approach supports both single components and complete engineering projects.

Our Vision

To become a globally trusted engineering and manufacturing partner, delivering innovative, high-precision and reliable solutions that enable industries to build a safer, stronger and more sustainable future.

Our Mission

To engineer and deliver world-class products and services through advanced technology, precision manufacturing, technical expertise and uncompromising quality, creating lasting value for our customers, employees, partners and society.

Quality & Certification

Certified management systems on every project.

Monocoque Pvt Ltd is certified to ISO 9001 (Quality Management), ISO 14001 (Environmental Management) and ISO 45001 (Occupational Health & Safety). Our documented systems cover inspection, testing, material traceability and Material Test Certificate (MTC) documentation, while protecting the environment and the people who work with us.
