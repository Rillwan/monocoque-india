Non-Destructive Testing (NDT/NDE)

Evaluate materials and welds without damaging them.

We provide and coordinate NDT/NDE services to support the evaluation of materials, welds, components and industrial equipment without causing damage to the items being examined.

VT

Visual Testing

Visual examination of materials, welds, components and fabricated assemblies.

PT

Penetrant Testing / Dye Penetrant Testing (PT / DPT)

Detection of surface-breaking discontinuities in suitable non-porous materials.

MT

Magnetic Particle Testing

Detection of surface and near-surface discontinuities in suitable ferromagnetic materials.

UT

Ultrasonic Testing

Examination of suitable materials and welds for internal discontinuities using ultrasonic techniques.

RT

Radiographic Testing

Examination of welds and components for internal discontinuities using radiographic techniques, subject to applicable radiation-safety requirements.

ET

Eddy Current Testing

Examination of electrically conductive materials for relevant discontinuities and material variations.

PMI

Positive Material Identification

Verification of alloy composition to help confirm material grades and identify potential material mix-ups in pipes, flanges, fittings, valves, welds and other industrial components.

UT-WT

UT Wall Thickness Measurement

Ultrasonic measurement of wall thickness in pipes, pressure vessels, tanks and other industrial equipment to assess thickness, monitor corrosion and support equipment integrity evaluations.
